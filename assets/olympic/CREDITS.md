# Olympic painting guide

- Work: **Hoh Rainforest, mossy logs photo by Fagerlund**, catalog reference `380-00002`.
- Photographer: Fagerlund, as identified in the image title; published by **Olympic National Park / National Park Service**.
- Source record: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Hoh_Rainforest,_mossy_log_tree_NPS_Photo_(16680304244).jpg
- Agency source page: https://www.flickr.com/photos/olympicnps/16680304244/
- Original download: https://live.staticflickr.com/7717/16680304244_062feeab41_o.jpg
- Terms: **public domain in the United States**, an NPS employee's official-duty work. The Commons record carries `PD US NPS`; its Flickr Public Domain Mark was verified by FlickreviewR on 22 November 2020. This is an item-specific determination, not a claim that all NPS-hosted images are reusable.
- Public Domain Mark: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/mark/1.0/
- NPS policy: https://www.nps.gov/aboutus/disclaimer.htm
- Agency publication date: 28 April 2015. An original exposure date is not established by the source record.
- Viewpoint: forest-floor interior in the **Hoh Rain Forest, Olympic National Park**, with moss-covered nurse logs/stumps, foreground ferns, and a diagonal fallen log. The source does not establish an exact Hall of Mosses trail location, so the scene does not make that narrower claim. No features from other photographs are combined.
- Preparation: the 5386 × 3464 original was proportionally reduced to a 1600 × 1029 JPEG guide. The painter center-cover crops the guide to its 1600 × 1000 logical canvas, removing about 14.5 guide pixels from the top and bottom. The original is not shipped.
- Runtime files: `guide.jpg` supplies colors and material boundaries to the sampled bristle painter. `painting.webp` is the original cached Canvas painting exported by the shared scene painter. The photograph is not drawn as the visible background.
- Changes: guide downsampling and center crop, restrained pigment grading, original multiscale brush marks, and animation of painted fern/moss cutouts with separately painted mist and sparse drips.

The guide is a fixed documented forest view, not live weather or trail-condition imagery.


Runtime assets: `painting.webp` is the finished original brush painting; `fallback.webp` is its smaller static fallback. `live-v1.webp` contains the painted motion cutouts and masks; `live-v1.json` records their source rectangles and movement parameters. The photographic guide remains local authoring material and is excluded from production. Normal visits do not download or sample it.
