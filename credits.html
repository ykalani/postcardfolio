Image credits

These landscapes are original brush paintings and animations built using photographic guides for geography and color.

Yosemite

Photographic guide by EF5, 2025. Tunnel View source. CC0 1.0.

Great Sand Dunes

Reference photograph supplied by the user. Painted adaptation of sunlit orange dune ridges, snow-covered mountain faces, sparse clouds, and open grassland. Photographer and license were not supplied.

Banff

Reference photograph supplied by the user. Painted adaptation of the mountain walls, turquoise lake, shoreline conifers, and canoes. The composition is cropped from the supplied portrait photograph.

Hawaiʻi Volcanoes

Photographic guide by B. Carr, USGS Hawaiian Volcano Observatory, October 10, 2021. USGS source. Public domain. The painting depicts this dated scene rather than current volcanic activity.

Pictured Rocks

Reference photograph supplied by the user, replacing the earlier Sleeping Bear sunset. The sandstone arch and kayaks match the Pictured Rocks shoreline. The landscape and kayakers are brush-painted from the supplied photograph. Kayaker silhouettes, proportions, and paddle positions are traced directly from that reference.

Beaumont Tower

Reference photograph supplied by the user. Original painted adaptation of the tower, magnolia blossoms, and evergreen branches.
