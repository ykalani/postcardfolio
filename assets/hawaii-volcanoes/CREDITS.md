# Hawaiʻi Volcanoes painting guide

- **Photograph:** October 10, 2021 — Kīlauea, Halema‘uma‘u lava lake at dusk.
- **Photographer:** B. Carr, USGS Hawaiian Volcano Observatory.
- **Reference date:** October 10, 2021.
- **Source page:** https://www.usgs.gov/media/images/october-10-2021-kilauea-halemaumau-lava-lake-dusk
- **Original download:** https://d9-wret.s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/assets/palladium/production/s3fs-public/thumbnails/image/previewImage-3358.jpg
- **License:** Public Domain, explicitly stated under “Sources/Usage” on the individual USGS source page; verified November 15, 2026.
- **Viewpoint:** West rim of Halemaʻumaʻu, looking into the crater at the western vent and lava lake. The source states that only the western half of the lake was active; the eastern half had a cooling solidified crust.
- **Preparation:** The source photograph was resized to a 1600 × 1067 JPEG guide. The renderer center-cover crops 33.5 vertical pixels into its 1600 × 1000 logical canvas. No landmarks are relocated. The full-resolution original is not shipped.
- **Art direction:** Charcoal crater walls are lifted slightly into blue-violet shadow; hot fractures remain concentrated in the actual active lake. The visible terrain is reconstructed with sampled original brush marks, not the photographic guide displayed as wallpaper. Gas motion is an artistic animation from the fixed photographed vent.

`guide.jpg` supplies geometry and pigment samples. `painting.webp` is a browser-baked cache of the original canvas brush painting. Animation depicts the documented 2021 scene; it does not report current volcanic activity and does not load a live webcam.


The detailed animation derives its hot-pixel masks and fracture tangents from this same guide. Gas emission origins follow the photographed west vent and smaller crater-margin gas sources; motion, texture, thermal shimmer, and slow incandescence variation are artistic interpretations. No live feed, extra landscape photograph, or unrelated eruption is composited into the scene. The static painting is exported after the shared fine-bristle pass.


Runtime assets: `painting.webp` is the finished original brush painting; `fallback.webp` is its smaller static fallback. `live-v1.webp` contains the painted motion cutouts and masks; `live-v1.json` records their source rectangles and movement parameters. The photographic guide remains local authoring material and is excluded from production. Normal visits do not download or sample it.


Current expressive-paint edition: `painting-v3.webp`, `fallback-v3.webp`, and `live-v3.*` use a coarse color study and broad, discrete-pigment flat-brush marks. These replace the earlier dense photo-like reconstruction. Original source provenance and any applicable source license above remain unchanged; see the shared playbook for the current rendering direction.

Current half-size brush edition: `painting-v4.webp`, `fallback-v4.webp`, and `live-v4.*` use marks half the previous edition’s length and breadth. Source provenance remains unchanged.
