These landscapes are original brush paintings and animations built using photographic guides for geography and color.

Yosemite

Photographic guide by EF5, 2025. Tunnel View source. CC0 1.0.

Great Sand Dunes

Photographic guide: NPS / Patrick Myers. October view north of the visitor center, with Medano Creek, dune ridges, and Cleveland Peak. NPS source and credit. Public-domain federal photograph.

Olympic

Hoh forest photographic guide by Fagerlund, published by Olympic National Park / NPS. Source and public-domain record.

Hawaiʻi Volcanoes

Photographic guide by B. Carr, USGS Hawaiian Volcano Observatory, October 10, 2021. USGS source. Public domain. The painting depicts this dated scene rather than current volcanic activity.

Sleeping Bear Dunes

Adapted from Sunset at Sleeping Bear Dunes by Ddbaran67, July 11, 2019, licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0. Changes include cropping, guide resizing, pigment grading, lens-ghost removal, original brush reconstruction, and animated water and vegetation. The adapted guide, derived painting, and Sleeping Bear scene artwork are distributed under the same CC BY-SA 4.0 license.
